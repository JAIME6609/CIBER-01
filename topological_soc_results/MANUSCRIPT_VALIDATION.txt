Headline executed results
-------------------------
- The logistic baseline achieved the highest in-distribution AUPRC: 0.933.
- The topology-guided model achieved AUPRC 0.909, ECE 0.054, and the smallest worst-case relative AUPRC loss: 11.4%.
- Topology fusion without consistency achieved F1 0.832 and MCC 0.807 in-distribution.
- The topology-guided model generated 14.7 alerts per shift, attained precision 1.000 in the top-risk decile, recovered 78.6% of incidents in the top twenty windows, and reached mean time to detect of 9.29 minutes under the stated assumptions.
- The evidence supports a bounded conclusion: topology improves stress stability and structural evidence in this experiment, but it does not dominate every nominal or operational metric.
